BLOODLINEAGE ENGINE: SYSTEMIC EXCLUSION, CRYPTOGRAPHIC PROVENANCE, DIGITAL LINEAGE, AND VERIFIABLE ACCOUNTABILITY

ABSTRACT
This work presents a critical examination of systemic exclusion and institutional gatekeeping within modern innovation frameworks through mathematical modelling, documentary analysis, cryptographic provenance, and a conceptual paradigm of creation.
The research examines circumstances in which an independent intellectual property creator may generate or hold economically significant intellectual assets while experiencing zero or severely constrained creator-level capital inflow and institutional support. A probability-gate model is developed to investigate how sequential requirements involving financial access, funding eligibility, legal representation, and institutional responsiveness may interact to substantially reduce an independent creator's probability of meaningful institutional participation.
In response to this study, the Bloodlineage Engine is introduced as a proposed decentralized cryptographic architecture for preserving provenance, asset traceability, digital lineage, public auditability, and creator-centred evidence.

1. INTRODUCTION
Intellectual property systems play an important role in recognizing, protecting, developing, and commercializing human creativity and innovation. However, the capacity to create valuable intellectual property and the capacity to obtain meaningful access to capital, legal representation, institutional recognition, regulatory processes, and commercialization infrastructure are not necessarily equivalent.
